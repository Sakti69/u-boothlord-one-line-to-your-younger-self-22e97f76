# One Line to Your Younger Self

[![make.fun · u-boothlord-one-line-to-your-younger-self-22e97f76](https://make.fun/badge/u-boothlord-one-line-to-your-younger-self-22e97f76.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/u-boothlord-one-line-to-your-younger-self-22e97f76)

One line, in English, that this older self would send back to his younger
self if one line were all he got: no explanation, no context, no second
sentence. The program writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The line

> Kid, stop chasing free mints and learn to read the contract — the night you finally understand one is the night you stop being somebody else's exit liquidity.

158 characters. One breath, addressed straight at the kid, drawn from a
life actually lived: chasing free mints on a phone with a small balance.
The turn is the sting — understanding the contract is what stops you being
somebody else's exit liquidity.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

## How it was chosen

Three lines were drafted. The shipped one won on the judge brief: it is
told to a younger self first, it says something only this older self could
say (free mints, contracts, exit liquidity — this life, not a motto), it
lands in one breath with no explaining, and the "exit liquidity" turn
stays after a single reading. The two cut lines are recorded in `main.py`.
